About nathmcraft

We are an independent game development studio dedicated to crafting focused, mechanics-first titles. We build games driven by clear systems, sharp pacing, and respect for player time.


Our roots are firmly planted in the indie scene. Alongside developing our own projects, we continue to playtest games for fellow creators — bringing honest critique, design insight, and actionable feedback to small teams and solo developers.

01 / Current Focus

Indie game development. Currently working on an unannounced roguelike with tight loops and high replay value.

02 / Design Philosophy

Mechanics first, zero filler. Clean controls, thoughtful progression, and distinctive atmosphere over marketing hype.

03 / Playtesting Roots

We know the indie struggle firsthand. We still run playtesting for indie titles, offering raw, actionable feedback from one developer to another.
