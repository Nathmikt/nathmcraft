About nathmcraft

At nathmcraft, we are dedicated exclusively to the indie gaming ecosystem. We believe that great indie games aren't born perfect—they are refined through rigorous, honest playtesting by people who understand design, pacing, and player frustration.


We partner directly with single developers and small teams to provide comprehensive feedback reports. Our testing cover-to-cover approach analyzes controls, level design clarity, progression pacing, and bugs, ensuring your game is polished and ready for the global stage.

01 / Our Focus

100% indie games. We focus on games with unique artistic vision, experimental mechanics, and passionate developers.

02 / What We Deliver

Detailed markdown reports covering gameplay flow, UI/UX pain points, visual/audio consistency, and repeatable bug logs.

03 / No Clichés

We keep our pipeline simple and direct. No excessive gaming jargon, no useless metrics. Just design analysis that helps you build a better game.
